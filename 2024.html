2024

In 2024, we went to many places and experienced new and fun things.


Spring Break - Sacramento

For spring break, we went to Sacramento! We went white water rafting, saw the eclipse, rail biking, Kung Fu Panda 4, disc golf, kayaking, snowshoeing, and mini golf. Jie did an internship this week.



Summer Vacation - Hawaii

For summer vacation, we flew to Hawaii! Our 2nd time in Hawaii. We stayed at Popo and Gong Gong's apartment. We spent a ton of time at the pool and ocean. We went to the top of Haleakala, a giant mountain. Inside Out 4. Toured a chocolate farm!



Holiday Break - LA + Disneyland

Happy Holidays! In my humble opinion, arguably the greatest trip ever! I love disneyland!!! Went to a Laker's game! K1 Speed! Disneyland for 4 days! Incredible! 3 days at Disneyland, 1 day at California Adventures. For the first time, I went on some crazy roller coasters!! We went on the new star wars rides! We tried to go on every single ride at Disneyland.
