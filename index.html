Amy

Some of my favorite family vacations have been our most recent Paris/London trip because it was our first big international trip together and I thought we did a great job traveling together and exploring the sites, our cruise vacations because all the entertainment is in one place and it's fun experiencing the ship together, plus the food is always fun and yummy, and our vacations at the beach like in Maui and Pismo because I like just laying out in the sun and reading and doing our sand castle contests. The things I like about our Seto family vacations are that we get to spend 24/7 together, we get to explore different places together and have that shared experience, and we make wonderful memories. There's nothing much I don't like about our vacations except when Aaron gets sick like in Florida and Paris or when we have to come home. A Seto family vacation means spending quality time together, trying new foods and experiences, and having fun! One place I would like to travel to next is the Mediterranean Sea area, preferably on a cruise. I love our family vacations and spending time together. What I'll always remember from our vacations are of course the big things like going to the Eiffel Tower or watching the fireworks on New Year's Eve at Disneyland, but also the little things like those big multi gallon tubs of ice cream that Daddy likes to get during vacations or our pit stops on the 5 freeway (like getting the Wendy's frosty or in the past, fudge), or you guys watching the elevator for people during the Embassy Suites breakfasts or the sauna bus in Paris. It's been fun to experience all those things together. I treasure all those memories. :)
