2026

In 2026, for ski week we went to Arizona!


Ski Week - Arizona

For ski week, we flew to Arizona! We saw Biosphere 2, Titan Missle Silo, and the Arizona Cardinals' State Farm Stadium! We also went to Activate. We even ate at Sweet Tomatoes!! Lastly, we visited the University of Arizona and Arizona State University.



Spring Break - Indiana + Chicago

For spring break, Aaron, Mommy, and Daddy visited Jie at Purdue! (Daddy was already at Purdue) We visited Indianapolis 500 Motor Speedway and Lucas Oil Stadium. We toured Purdue, Northwestern, and Loyola Chicago. We checked out Fair Oaks Farm and Albanese gummy bears. We watched Project Hail Mary! We visited the Art Institute of Chicago, Frank Lloyd Wright House, and Shedd Aquarium. We saw the famous Bean!



Summer Vacation - Spain, France, Italy + Cruise

For summer vacation, the Seto family visited Europe again! This time we traveled to Barcelona, Rome, and took a Norwegian cruise to Spain, France, and Italy. We took a walking tour of Barcelona. We saw the world famous Sagrada Familia church. On the Norwegian Epic we sailed to Marseille, Villefranche and Nice, Pisa, Messina, and Pompeii + Salerno! In Rome, we saw the Spanish Steps, Trevi Fountain, Pantheon, Colosseum, Roman Forum, and St. Peter's Basilica. We also ate OG alfredo pasta and Roman pizza.
