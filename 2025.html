2025

In 2025, we went to many places and experienced new and fun things.


Ski Week - LA and San Diego

For ski week, we headed down to LA. We visited the La Brea Tar Pits, cemetery, the Lai's House, and the Grove. For Jie's college search, we visited University of San Diego, UC San Diego, and UCLA.



Summer Vacation - Paris and London

For summer vacation, we went to Paris and London! It was Jie and Aaron's first time out of North America and first time in Europe. In Paris we saw the Notre Dame, Arc de Triomphe, Champs-Elysees, Segway Tour, Eiffel Tower, Louvre, Eiffel Tower, Opera House, Musee D'Orsay and took a photoshoot. In London we saw the Harry Potter Studio Tour, Westminster Abbey, Six the Musical, Tower of London, Kensington Palace, Science Museum, and Chelsea Stadium Tour.
